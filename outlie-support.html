Common questions

How does the daily puzzle work?

Everyone gets the same three puzzles each day, chosen automatically by date. The day rolls over at midnight UTC.

What are Compass Tokens?

Tokens pay for hints. You get a free allowance every day. You can earn more by watching an optional rewarded video, or remove the limit entirely with Outlie Pro.

My streak reset — why?

A day only counts toward your streak when you solve all of that day's puzzles. Missing a day resets the streak. Solving an older puzzle from the archive does not change your streak either way.

I bought Pro but it isn't showing

Open the paywall and choose Restore purchases, making sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID you bought it with. If it still doesn't appear, email us.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by us: open the iOS Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Cancelling stops future renewals and Pro stays active until the end of the paid period.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

If you're in a region where this applies, tap Privacy options at the bottom of the home screen. See the privacy policy for detail.

I watched an ad but didn't get my reward

Rewards are confirmed by Google's servers and can take a moment. If nothing arrives, check your connection and try once more — you won't be charged tokens for a failed reward. If it keeps happening, email us.
